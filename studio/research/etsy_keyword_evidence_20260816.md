# Etsy Keyword Evidence — 2026-08-16

Two methods were used on the same day, and they disagreed. Read the second one.

1. **Etsy autosuggest.** Each seed phrase was typed into the buyer search box on `etsy.com/se-en`.
   Etsy builds that list from real queries.
2. **eRank Bulk Keyword Tool.** 40 phrases, in two runs of 20, marketplace Etsy, country USA.

> **The lesson: autosuggest shows that a phrase exists, not that anyone searches it.**
> `sunrise sunset wall art` produced five autosuggest variants. eRank measures it at **under 20
> searches a month, against 18,652 competing listings, keyword difficulty 100.** Autosuggest sent
> the first rewrite of both titles in the wrong direction. Use eRank for any title decision.

## The rule this produced

**The noun phrase decides everything.** The same subject wins or loses by its last two words.

| Loser | | Winner | |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scandinavian print` | 10 | `scandinavian wall art` | 1,200 |
| `personalized map` | 10 | `custom map print` | 1,200 |
| `sunrise print` | 10 | `sunset wall art` | 1,150 |
| `wall art personalized` | 0 | `personalized wall art` | 2,350 |

Always test the variants before committing a title.

---

## Method limits

- **Country is USA.** The shop sells to the EU and the US. Swedish and German volumes are not
  measured here.
- **Free-tier eRank blurs most columns.** Only *Search Trend*, and the first row of each run, show
  numbers. Competitor count and keyword difficulty came from the one unblurred row per run.
- **The free plan allows 5 runs a day.** Batch 20 keywords per run.
- **True organic rank stays unverified.** A logged-in seller session shows the seller's own listings
  near the top of page 1; a fresh browser context is blocked by Etsy bot protection. Never read rank
  while signed in to Shop Manager.

### The finding that costs the most

**The product's own name is not a search term.** `personalized playground map` measures 0, and
`personalized daylight poster` measures 0. Both sit in the middle of a live title. The earlier note
above accepted a zero-volume niche phrase because it has no competition. Etsy search sent **0 of
the 7 visits** this month, so that trade is not paying. A phrase with no volume and no competition
returns no impressions at all.

`minimalist wall art` at 2,210 is the highest-volume replacement measured for either product, and
it describes both honestly.

**No title was changed.** `BL-002` is `needs-review`. The founder decides.
