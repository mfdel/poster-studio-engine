# HopscotchMaps — Etsy Traffic Roadmap

*Companion to [research_etsy_traffic_tips_20260801.md](research_etsy_traffic_tips_20260801.md). Sized for a
side hustle: low hours, low risk, compounding payoff. Ordered so the free, high-leverage work comes first
and paid ads come only after the listings are proven to convert.*

## Guiding principle

You will never out-volume a mass POD seller. You win on **(1) an ownable niche nobody targets
("personalized playground map"), (2) genuinely original hand-illustrated work Etsy's 2025 rules reward,
and (3) photography + Pinterest that make every listing convert and compound.** Do the free stuff
relentlessly before spending a krona on ads.

---

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Week 1) · *free, ~4–6 hrs*

The listing has to be a conversion machine before traffic is worth chasing.

- [ ] **Install eRank (Free/Basic $5.99)** — validate keyword demand vs. competition for your niche.
- [x] **Rewrite titles with the personalized-gift formula**: `product + recipient + occasion + style`.
      Front-load the primary phrase in the first 3–5 words (e.g. *"Personalized Playground Map — Custom
      Family Keepsake, New Baby Gift, Nursery Wall Art"*).
- [x] **Fill all 13 tags** with multi-word phrases (≤20 chars), each covering a *different* search —
      occasion, recipient, room, style. Don't repeat title words.
- [x] **Description**: primary keyword in the first sentence (helps Google too), then answer the 4 buyer
      questions (what's customizable, what info you need, sizes/materials, delivery timeline). Short
      paragraphs, warm CTA.
- [x] **Turn on Share & Save** and save your trackable shop/listing links — use them everywhere from now on.
- [x] **Set message auto-reply + a personal 24h response habit** (feeds Star Seller + shop quality).

## Phase 1 — Photography overhaul (Weeks 1–2) · *free–low, highest ROI*

Lifestyle mockups = ~2x click-through, and CTR now drives rank.

- [x] **Fill every photo slot** with the map **already personalized** (real family name + real
      playgrounds), never a blank template.
- [ ] **In-room framed mockups**: nursery, playroom, hallway walls — not plain backgrounds. Use free/low
      mockup templates; batch one design across many room scenes.
- [ ] **Clean, bright, centered hero** at ~2600×2000 landscape so Etsy's square crop and Etsy Ads don't
      clip frame edges.
- [ ] Add a short **process/detail shot** (close-up of illustration style) to reinforce "original,
      hand-made."

## Phase 2 — Pinterest engine (Weeks 2–8, then ongoing) · *free, the compounding channel*

Expect a 3–6 month lag, then traffic that compounds. Start now so it's warm by autumn/holiday.

- [ ] **Convert to a Pinterest Business account**, verify your website, set brand board covers.
- [ ] **Batch 30–50 fresh pins** in Canva: 5–10 distinct graphics per listing (angles, room scenes,
      text overlays like "A keepsake map of your family's playgrounds").
- [ ] **Schedule 1/day (min 2–3/week)** via Pinterest's native scheduler; link each pin to the product
      page using your **Share & Save** link.
- [ ] **Keywords in first 50–100 chars** of every pin description; research terms in Pinterest's search bar.
- [ ] Add a few **10s video pins** (illustration process) — algorithm favors them.
- [ ] Track **outbound clicks, saves, impressions**; if impressions are high but clicks low, it's a
      design problem — iterate the graphic.

## Phase 3 — One video channel + package insert (Weeks 4–10) · *free, secondary*

- [ ] Pick **one** of TikTok *or* Instagram Reels (whichever you'll actually keep up). Post **weekly**:
      raw "watch me hand-illustrate a family's playground map" process clips, hook in first 3 seconds,
      cross-post to the other as Reels/Shorts.
- [ ] **Design a package insert** (thank-you card + QR code) pointing to a simple email signup landing
      page with a lead magnet (a free printable "Our Playground Adventures" page). Include from order #1.

## Phase 4 — Small, disciplined Etsy Ads (only after listings convert) · *paid, capped*

Don't start until Phases 0–1 are done and you have a few organic sales proving the listing converts.

- [ ] Start **$1–$3/day**, capped at **5–10% of gross monthly revenue**.
- [ ] Advertise **only your 1–3 best/most-complete listings**.
- [ ] **Let it run 30 days** before judging (7–14 days just to optimize).
- [ ] **Kill rules**: pause a listing at **$30 spend / 0 orders**; pause anything **under 2x ROAS** after
      30 days. Target **≥3x ROAS**. Garden weekly — bump winners, cut burners.
- [ ] **Offsite Ads: opt out for now** (you're under $10k/yr). Revisit as you approach $10k (mandatory there).

## Phase 5 — Trust & seasonality (ongoing) · *free*

- [ ] Chase **Star Seller**: 95% <24h replies, 4.8+ rating, 95% on-time shipping, ≥5 orders. Your
      made-to-order human checkpoint makes this achievable — use it.
- [ ] **Ask for reviews** politely in your delivery message / insert; reviews are your slowest-accruing,
      highest-value signal in this niche.
- [ ] **Plan gift seasons backward**: baby-shower/new-baby demand is year-round; layer Christmas,
      Mother's/Father's Day, first-birthday angles into pins and tags ~6–8 weeks ahead.

## What to do this week (if you do nothing else)

1. Rewrite titles + 13 tags with the **personalized-gift formula** (Phase 0).
2. Reshoot the listing with **in-room framed, already-personalized mockups** (Phase 1).
3. Turn on **Share & Save** and start using the links (Phase 0).
4. Set up **Pinterest Business** and schedule your first 20 fresh pins (Phase 2).

Everything above is free. Ads (Phase 4) wait until those four prove the listing converts.

## Metrics to watch (Etsy Stats + Pinterest Analytics)

| Signal | Why it matters | Healthy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Listing **visits → favorites** | Feeds Listing Quality Score / CTR | Rising favorites-per-visit |
| **Conversion rate** (visits → orders) | Now outranks keyword perfection | Trend up after photo/title fixes |
| Pinterest **outbound clicks** | Your main external traffic | Climbs from ~month 3 |
| **Star Seller** criteria | Trust + search filter inclusion | All green each month |
| Etsy Ads **ROAS** (if running) | Ad profitability | ≥3x; cut <2x |
