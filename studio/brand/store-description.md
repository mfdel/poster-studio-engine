## 3. About / Story section (the "About" tab — this is where the brand lives)

**Headline:** *A map of all the little places you'll grow up loving.*

**Story:**

> It started with my daughter and a simple question every parent knows: *"Where should we go today?"*
>
> I realized that choosing a playground could be more than a decision I made in my head in ten
> seconds — it could be *ours*. So I made her a map: every playground near our home, drawn warm and
> friendly, with space beside each one to give it stars, scribble a note, or stick a photo from the
> day we went.
>
> Now picking where to play is a little ritual we do together at the map on the wall. And slowly it's
> filling up — with stickers, wobbly drawings, and snapshots of the exact places she's growing up in.
> One day the swings will be too small for her. This map won't be.
>
> **That's what Hopscotch Maps makes:** a personalized, hand-illustrated map of *your* family's
> playgrounds — a keepsake your child co-creates, and a gentle screen-free ritual you'll actually
> look forward to.
>
> **How it works:** Tell us your address and how far you'd like to roam. We hand-style a beautiful map
> of the playgrounds around you, with a numbered list, a star-rating legend, and blank spots for notes,
> drawings, and photo corners. You choose:
>
> - 🖼️ **Digital file, made to order** — a print-ready PDF (300+ DPI) in standard sizes, hand-styled
>   for you and delivered within 2–3 days. Print it at home or at a local shop, framed the way you like.
> - 📦 **Printed poster** — we print it in your country and ship it, ready for the wall.
> - 🪵 **Framed poster** — the print in a real frame (wood or white), ready to hang.
>
> **A note, honestly:** the digital option is a file, not a physical item — nothing is mailed. Printed
> and framed maps ship after you approve a preview. Colors can vary a little between screens, printers,
> and paper. We'd rather tell you up front. 💛
>
> Made by a dad in Sweden, for families everywhere. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
