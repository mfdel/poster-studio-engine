# Logo Prompts — 5 Alternatives

Five **distinct** logo concepts, all sharing the brand DNA: warm, hand-illustrated, kid-friendly,
storybook — the opposite of the clinical/minimalist incumbents. Each prompt is written to drop into
Midjourney, DALL·E, Ideogram (best for text), or a similar tool.

**Shared brand notes to keep consistent across all five:**
- **Palette:** warm cream `#FBF6EC`, meadow green `#7FB069`, sky blue `#8ECAE6`, sunshine yellow
  `#F4C95D`, soft coral `#F08A7B`, warm charcoal ink `#3D3A35`.
- **Feel:** hand-drawn linework, gentle imperfection, cozy, friendly — a children's-book illustration,
  not a corporate mark.
- **Must work small:** legible as a favicon / Etsy avatar (a busy design fails at 32px).
- **Format tips:** ask for *flat vector, solid background, generous negative space, centered*. For text
  logos, **Ideogram** renders lettering far better than Midjourney. Generate the icon and wordmark
  separately so you can lock the icon (which scales down) and set the type cleanly later.
- **Swap the name:** replace `Hopscotch Maps` with your chosen shop name.

> ⚠️ AI logo tools often garble text and may echo other brands. Treat outputs as **concept
> directions**, then have the final mark cleaned up / redrawn as true vector (or by a designer) so it's
> crisp, unique, and trademark-safe.

## Logo 1 — "Pin + Swing" combination mark (recommended lead)

**Concept:** A classic map location pin whose round top holds a tiny playground swing — instantly
says *map + playground* in one friendly symbol.

```
A charming hand-illustrated logo icon: a rounded map location pin, and inside the circular
top of the pin a tiny child's swing hangs from the top edge, gently swinging. Warm children's-
storybook illustration style, soft hand-drawn linework with slightly imperfect edges, flat
vector, minimal detail so it stays legible when small. Color palette: meadow green and sky blue
pin, sunshine-yellow swing seat, warm charcoal outline, on a solid warm-cream background.
Cozy, playful, friendly, wholesome. Centered, generous negative space, simple, iconic,
sticker-like. No gradients, no photorealism, no drop shadows, no text.
--style raw --ar 1:1
```
**Negative / avoid:** clutter, harsh corporate geometry, neon colors, 3D, realistic photo, text.

## Logo 2 — "Dotted path through the park" scene badge

**Concept:** A small circular badge showing a whimsical dotted trail winding between two or three
tiny playground elements (a slide, a tree, a swing set) — the "let's pick where to go" ritual as a
tiny illustrated map.

```
A cozy circular badge logo: a small hand-drawn map scene inside a soft circle, with a curving
dashed path (like a treasure-map trail) winding between three tiny playground icons — a little
slide, a rounded tree, and a swing set. Warm children's-book illustration, hand-drawn wobbly
linework, flat vector, limited palette of meadow green, sky blue, sunshine yellow and soft coral
on warm cream, warm charcoal outlines. Playful, nostalgic, keepsake feel. Simple enough to read
at small sizes, centered, clean border, lots of breathing room. No text, no gradients, no 3D,
no photorealism.
--style raw --ar 1:1
```
**Negative / avoid:** too many icons, tiny details that vanish when scaled, gradients, realism.

## Logo 3 — "Little explorer with a map" character mark

**Concept:** A tiny, gender-neutral child holding an oversized folded map, mid-step — the co-creation
/ family-adventure story in a single warm character. Great for a brand that leans on the founder story.

```
A sweet character logo: a small gender-neutral toddler seen from a simple front angle, holding an
oversized folded paper map that is bigger than they are, taking a happy step forward. Warm
children's-picture-book illustration, soft rounded shapes, hand-drawn linework with gentle
imperfection, flat vector. Palette: warm cream background, meadow-green and sky-blue clothing,
sunshine-yellow map, soft coral accents, warm charcoal outlines. Wholesome, tender, playful,
keepsake mood. Kept simple and iconic so it reads at small sizes. Centered, generous negative
space. No text, no gradients, no 3D render, no photorealism, no facial detail beyond two dot eyes
and a small smile.
--style raw --ar 1:1
```
**Negative / avoid:** detailed faces, specific ethnicity/gender cues, busy background, realism.

## Logo 4 — Hand-lettered wordmark + tiny pin dot (text-forward)

**Concept:** A friendly hand-lettered logotype where the dot of an `i` (or the `o`) becomes a little
map pin or a sun. Clean, boutique, scales beautifully across a shop banner and avatar. **Use Ideogram
for this one** — it renders lettering reliably.

```
A hand-lettered logotype reading "Hopscotch Maps" in a warm, friendly, slightly bouncy rounded
script with gentle hand-drawn imperfection. The dot above the letter "i" is replaced by a tiny
map location pin (or a small sun). Two-line stacked layout allowed. Children's-storybook,
boutique, keepsake brand feel. Warm charcoal lettering on a warm-cream background, with a single
sunshine-yellow and meadow-green accent on the pin. Flat vector, crisp, high legibility, clean
kerning, centered, plenty of white space. No gradients, no 3D, no busy background, no extra icons.
--ar 3:2
```
**Negative / avoid:** misspelled/garbled letters (regenerate until clean), thin ornate script that
fails when small, more than one accent color.

## Logo 5 — "Compass rose made of playground pieces" emblem

**Concept:** A cartography compass rose where the four points are built from playground silhouettes
(slide, swing, seesaw, sandcastle/tree) — leans into the "map" heritage with a clever, ownable twist.
Slightly more premium/timeless; works well embossed on the printed poster corner.

```
An emblem logo: a simple, elegant compass rose where the four cardinal points are subtly formed
from tiny playground silhouettes — a slide, a swing, a seesaw, and a small tree or sandcastle.
Warm hand-illustrated cartography style with gentle hand-drawn linework, flat vector, symmetrical
and balanced. Palette: warm charcoal and meadow green line art with sunshine-yellow and sky-blue
accents on a warm-cream background. Timeless, keepsake, storybook-map feel — clever but clean and
uncluttered so it reads at small sizes. Centered, generous negative space. No text, no gradients,
no 3D, no photorealism.
--style raw --ar 1:1
```
**Negative / avoid:** over-ornate compass detailing, so many elements it turns to mush when small,
metallic 3D, realism.

## How to use these (quick workflow)

1. **Generate 4–8 variations per prompt**, then shortlist the 2–3 concepts that read clearly at
   **avatar size** (shrink each to ~64px and squint — the winner survives).
2. **Test on cream + on a photo** (a poster on a wall) — the mark needs to work on both.
3. **Lock one icon + one wordmark** so you have: (a) a square avatar, (b) a horizontal banner logo,
   (c) a tiny corner mark for the printed poster.
4. **Redraw the winner as clean vector** (SVG) so it's sharp at any size, editable, and unique enough
   to be trademark-safe — don't ship a raw AI raster as your final mark.
