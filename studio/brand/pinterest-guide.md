# Pinterest Guide , Hopscotch Maps

> Paste-ready, like [etsy-listing-READY.md](etsy-listing-READY.md). Swap the shop name if you
> landed on a different one. Everything here assumes traffic goes to the **Etsy listing** ,
> there is no own-store yet, and that's fine. Pinterest works perfectly as an Etsy feeder.

**Why Pinterest for this product:** nursery decor, baby-shower gifts, keepsakes and wall art are
Pinterest's core categories, and the audience is overwhelmingly the person who buys this gift.
A pin keeps driving traffic for months; an Instagram post dies in six hours. It is also the only
free channel where **every pin carries a clickable link**.

---

## 0. Terminology

**Pinterest is a search engine that looks like a scrapbook.** People arrive typing queries
("nursery wall art", "baby shower gift") the way they'd use Google , not to socialise. Hence
keywords matter enormously and followers barely matter at all.

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Pin** | One image + title + description + **a link**. A visual bookmark. Yours link to the Etsy listing. |
| **Board** | A folder holding pins, organised by topic. `New Baby Gifts` is a board; the pins inside are pins. |
| **Save** (old: *repin*) | Someone adds your pin to *their* board , Pinterest's main sharing action, and how a pin travels beyond your own followers. |
| **Board section** | A sub-folder inside a board. Ignore for now. |
| **Fresh pin** | A pin using an image Pinterest has never seen. Heavily favoured; each gets its own distribution test , the reason to make 5–10 *different* images per listing. |
| **Product / Rich Pin** | Auto-pulls live price + title from the destination. Automatic on Etsy links (see §1). |
| **Home feed** | The algorithmic endless scroll on opening the app. |
| **Related pins** | The grid under a pin someone clicked. Real traffic, not directly controllable. |
| **Impressions** | Times your pin appeared on a screen. Mostly vanity. |
| **Outbound clicks** 🔑 | Clicks through to your Etsy listing. **The only metric that matters.** |
| **Pin Builder** | Pinterest's creation form, and where the free native scheduler lives. |
| **Followers** | Near-irrelevant. Distribution comes from keywords and boards, not audience. Don't chase. |

> **The one relationship to remember:** a pin's reach depends on its own keywords **and the board
> it sits on**. Pinterest reads the board title to classify the pin. Boards first, then pins.

## 2. Boards (create all 8 today, before pinning anything)

Pinterest reads the **board title** to decide what a pin is about. A great pin on a vague board
gets mis-filed and dies. Board names must be plain search phrases, not cute brand language.

| Board name 🔑 | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Personalized Playground Map` | Your money board , every product pin |
| `New Baby Gifts` | Highest-intent gift buyer |
| `Baby Shower Gift Ideas` | Second-highest, very seasonal |
| `Nursery Wall Art & Decor` | Where the poster physically ends up |
| `First Birthday Gift Ideas` | Underrated, strong buying intent |
| `Family Keepsakes & Memory Ideas` | The ritual framing, your actual moat |
| `Screen-Free Activities for Toddlers` | Top-of-funnel, brings the right parent |
| `Playground Adventures` | Lifestyle/inspiration , the reason to visit parks |

## 3. Pin images , what you already have, what you need

You have nine images in [posters/fam001-playground-map/brand/listing-photos/](listing-photos/) at **2000×2500 (4:5)**. Pinterest
displays 4:5 fine, so **you can start pinning tonight with zero new work.** But **2:3 (1000×1500 or
2000×3000) is the ratio Pinterest optimizes for** , it occupies more vertical space in the feed,
which is the whole game. Taller than 2:3 gets truncated.

**Do both:** pin the existing 4:5 set this week, and generate a proper 2:3 set from
[studio/assets/make_listing_mockups.py](../assets/make_listing_mockups.py) , it already has `W, H = 2000, 2500`
at line 26 and an on-brand palette, so a 2:3 output mode is a small change, not a rebuild.

**Target: 5–10 distinct pin images per listing.** Not the same image ten times , different
crops, different text overlays, different angles. Pinterest treats each new image as a *fresh pin*
and gives it its own distribution test.

**Pin design rules for this product:**
- **Text overlay on the top third**, large enough to read on a phone at thumbnail size. Most pins
  are judged at ~200px wide.
- Lead with the *emotional* frame, not the spec: "A map of every playground you'll grow up loving"
  beats "300 DPI printable PDF".
- Your **whimsy** style is the one that stops a scroll , lead with it. Minimal reads as generic
  map art and competes directly with Mapiful.
- Show the map **in a room** more often than flat. Nursery context is what the buyer is imagining.
- The **before/after ritual** (blank map → map covered in stars, drawings, photos) is your single
  most Pinterest-native image and you don't have it yet. Make it once you've printed your own.

## 4. Paste-ready pins

**Title format:** keyword phrase first, ≤40 chars visible.
**Description:** 2–3 natural sentences, keywords woven in, ~200 chars is plenty. No hashtag spam ,
2–3 max, and they matter far less than they do on Instagram.

### Destination link 🔑 , always the Share & Save link

Etsy's **Share & Save** programme credits **4% of the order value** back against the Etsy bill when
the buyer arrives through a share link. That link is the **shop-subdomain** form, not `www.etsy.com`:

```
Shop     https://hopscotchmaps.etsy.com
Listing  https://hopscotchmaps.etsy.com/se-en/listing/4547086425/personalized-playground-map-custom
```

Every pin, profile link, and any other outbound link we publish anywhere uses this form. UTM
parameters can be appended freely (`?utm_source=Pinterest&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=<pin>`) ,
the credit is carried by the subdomain, not the query string. A `www.etsy.com/listing/...` link
still sells, it just forfeits the 4%.

## 5. Cadence

**3–5 pins per day is the 2026 sweet spot** , quality over volume, consistency over bursts.
Pinterest's algorithm rewards steady posting far more than it rewards any single great pin.

**Realistic side-hustle version:** batch **20–25 pins on a Sunday**, schedule them across the week
using Pinterest's own free native scheduler (up to 30 days out , you don't need Tailwind yet).
That's about 45 minutes a week.

Each pin needs: image → title → description → **destination link** (the Share & Save listing URL,
not the shop homepage) → correct board.

**Seasonality to plan around:** baby-shower and new-baby searches are steady year-round, but
Pinterest users plan **45–60 days ahead**. Christmas gift pins need to be live by **early October**;
Mother's/Father's Day pins about six weeks out. Pin the Christmas set before you think you should.

## 6. What not to do

- **Don't link every pin to your shop homepage.** Link to the specific listing. Fewer clicks to buy.
- **Don't paste a `www.etsy.com` link.** It throws away the Share & Save 4% credit , use the
  `hopscotchmaps.etsy.com` form every time.
- **Don't repost the identical image** to five boards on the same day. That's the old strategy;
  it now reads as spam. One image → one board → its best-fit board.
- **Don't buy followers or use engagement pods.** Follower count is close to irrelevant on
  Pinterest , it's a search engine, not a social network. Distribution comes from keywords.
- **Don't put a watermark or URL across the middle** of the image. It suppresses saves.
- **Don't expect anything for 6–10 weeks.** Pinterest is genuinely slow to start and then
  compounds. This is the main reason people quit it right before it works.
- **Don't run Pinterest Ads yet.** Your ad money belongs on Etsy Ads while the shop is cold ,
  Etsy Ads reach people already in checkout mode. Revisit Pinterest Ads when something is
  organically proven.

## 8. What to measure

Only one number matters at the start: **outbound clicks to Etsy**. Impressions and saves feel good
and buy nothing.

- Pinterest Analytics → **Outbound clicks**, last 30 days
- Etsy Shop Manager → **Stats → Traffic sources → Pinterest** (the number that actually reconciles)

**Realistic expectation:** near-zero for 4–6 weeks, then slow compounding. If after 90 days of
consistent pinning you have under ~50 outbound clicks/month, the pin *images* are the problem, not
the strategy , go back to section 3 and make the room-context and ritual shots.
