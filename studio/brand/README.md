## Brand in one line

> A warm, hand-illustrated **keepsake map of a family's local playgrounds** — a child co-creates it
> by rating parks, drawing, and sticking photos. Sold as a **personalized new-parent / nursery gift**,
> digital-first with a print-on-demand poster upsell.

## Brand pillars (the "why we're different")

1. **Warm & hand-illustrated, not clinical.** Incumbents (Mapiful, Grafomap) are minimalist and
   adult. We are kid-friendly, cozy, and storybook-like. *The art style is the moat.*
2. **A ritual, not a finder.** The map is a canvas for "let's pick where to go today" and a childhood
   keepsake — not a utility to locate a park.
3. **A gift with a heart.** Positioned for baby showers, first birthdays, and new parents. The founder
   story — *"I made this for my daughter"* — is the authentic voice.

## Brand voice

- **Tone:** warm, gentle, playful, sincere. Talks to parents like a fellow parent, not a marketer.
- **Do:** use words like *keepsake, ritual, together, little, adventures, grow, our places.*
- **Don't:** sound techy or clinical (avoid "OSM," "geodata," "algorithm" in customer-facing copy),
  over-promise ("find the best playground"), or use hype/emoji-spam.

## Suggested color & type direction (for logo + shop)

- **Palette:** soft, sunny, and tactile — warm cream paper `#FBF6EC`, meadow green `#7FB069`,
  sky blue `#8ECae6`, sunshine yellow `#F4C95D`, soft coral `#F08A7B`, warm charcoal ink `#3D3A35`.
- **Type:** a friendly hand-drawn/rounded display for the logo + a clean humanist sans for body.
- **Texture:** subtle paper grain, hand-drawn linework, gentle imperfection (nothing sharp/corporate).
