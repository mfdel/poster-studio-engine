# Product Display & Photography Ideas

Ideas for listing photos, mockups, and Pinterest pins. On Etsy the **first photo wins the click** and
the rest tell the story. For a keepsake/gift product the emotion sells — so mix clean product shots
with warm *human ritual* shots (like a parent and child choosing a park together).

**Golden rules for this product:**
- **Shot #1 = the hero.** A framed poster on a nicely styled wall, clearly showing it's a *playground*
  map (title + a few park illustrations legible). Bright, clean, aspirational.
- **Shots #2–4 = emotion & use.** The ritual (choosing together), the child annotating, gift context.
- **Shot #2 = the format picker.** A clean card showing the three ways to buy — **Digital · Printed
  poster · Framed poster** with prices — so buyers grasp the choice before they open the dropdown
  (generated as `09-three-formats.png`).
- **Shots #5–9 = the details & the offer.** Close-ups of the rating stars/annotation zones, the size
  chart, "what you get," the personalization options, and the honest note (digital = a file; prints &
  frames ship to you; colors vary).
- Keep a **consistent warm palette + light** across all photos so the shop grid looks like one brand.
- Every printed map in a photo should show the small **© OpenStreetMap contributors** attribution.

---

## A. The "ritual" lifestyle shots (your differentiator — lead with these)

1. **Father & daughter choosing a park.** ⭐ Your idea, and the single best shot. A dad crouched to
   his daughter's height beside the framed map on the wall; she points at a playground, he looks where
   she's pointing. Warm morning light, cozy home, both a little candid (not staring at camera). Caption
   overlay optional: *"Where should we go today?"* This *is* the brand in one image.

2. **Mother & toddler at kid-height.** The map hung low, at child eye-level; toddler on tiptoes
   touching a park illustration, parent kneeling beside. Reinforces "made for the child, hung for the
   child."

3. **The whole family deciding.** Two kids + a parent gathered at the map, one kid mid-laugh pointing.
   Sells the "family ritual" and works for the gift angle.

4. **Adding a sticker/star after the outing.** Close, over-the-shoulder shot of a small hand pressing
   a gold star sticker next to a playground on the map — the "we went there" moment. Shows the map is
   *alive* and co-created, not just décor.

5. **Drawing on the map.** A child's hand with a crayon adding a wobbly doodle in an annotation box.
   Emphasizes the keepsake/co-creation promise.

6. **Sticking a photo.** A tiny printed snapshot being tucked into a photo corner on the map. Bridges
   to the future physical gift-kit tier.

7. **Before-the-outing scene.** Map on the wall by the front door; shoes/stroller in frame; child
   pointing on the way out. Tells the "part of our routine" story.

---

## B. Clean product / wall shots (the "hero" + trust builders)

8. **Framed on a styled wall.** The poster framed in light oak, on a warm neutral wall, with a small
   plant, a shelf, or a stroller nearby for a "family home" feel. This is your likely **Photo #1**.

9. **Nursery context.** The map above a crib or a low bookshelf in a soft, pastel nursery — nails the
   *new-baby / nursery gift* positioning that drives proven demand.

10. **Kids' room context.** Above a play corner with a rug and toy baskets — shows it lives where the
    child plays.

11. **Three-size line-up.** The same map shown at A4 / A3 / A2 side by side (or a person holding one
    for scale) so buyers understand size — a top Etsy question you can pre-empt.

12. **Held-in-hands / unrolled.** Hands holding the rolled or flat print near a window — tactile,
    conveys real paper quality for the poster SKU.

---

## C. Detail & information shots (close the sale)

13. **Annotation-zone close-up.** Macro of the numbered park list + star-rating legend + a blank note
    box, so buyers *get* the co-creation feature at a glance.

14. **Personalization showcase.** A tidy graphic: same map in 2–3 color themes + a custom title
    ("Sofia's Playground Map") + the home marker — shows it's truly personalized.

15. **"What you get" graphic.** Simple, on-brand text card: *"Hand-styled, made to order · delivered
    in 2–3 days · print-ready PDF at 300+ DPI · A4/A3/A2 + US sizes · print at home or local shop."*
    For the digital SKU — never say "instant".

16. **How-it-works, 3 steps.** Numbered mini-illustration: *1) Send your address & radius → 2) We
    hand-style your map → 3) Download & print / we ship it.*

17. **The honest note card.** A gentle on-brand card: *"Digital is a file you print — nothing is
    mailed; printed & framed maps ship to you; colors vary slightly by screen, printer & paper."*
    Builds trust and cuts refund/review risk.

18. **Gift framing card.** *"A keepsake gift for new parents, baby showers & first birthdays"* over a
    softly styled gift-wrapped mock. Targets the higher-intent gift buyer.

## D. Mockup & production tips (do it cheaply, look premium)

- **Digital mockups first (zero cost).** Drop your real generated map into free frame/interior
  mockups (e.g. `Smartmockups`, `Placeit`, Canva frames) to launch before you print anything physical.
- **One real print for the hero.** Order a single Gelato poster of your daughter's actual map, frame
  it, and shoot the ritual shots at home — authentic + on-brand, and doubles as your first product.
- **Consistent light & palette.** Shoot near a window in soft daylight; keep the warm cream/green/blue
  palette in props so the grid feels cohesive.
- **Faces optional / privacy-safe.** The ritual shots work with hands, backs, and side profiles — no
  need for identifiable faces, which keeps it comfortable to use your own family.
- **Pinterest crop.** Also export tall **2:3 vertical** versions of the ritual + nursery shots with a
  short text overlay — Pinterest is the top free channel for nursery/printable buyers.
- **Video/GIF (bonus).** A 5–10s clip of a child adding a star or pointing at the map massively lifts
  Etsy conversion — a phone clip is enough.
