# Gemini Photo Prompts — Lifestyle Listing Shots

Photoreal room / ritual photos for the Etsy listing, generated on the **Gemini website**
(gemini.google.com → image mode / "Nano Banana"). These are the *emotional* shots that sell a
keepsake; the pixel-sharp graphic cards live in `posters/fam001-playground-map/brand/listing-photos/` (made separately).

## How to use
1. Open a chat, click the **+ / image** button and **upload the map** named under each shot
   (default: `brand/our_playground_map_whimsy_A2_clean_portrait.png` — the warm one sells best).
2. Paste the prompt. Ask for **portrait 4:5** (Etsy's grid ratio). Generate **2–3 variations**, keep the best.
3. Save into `posters/fam001-playground-map/brand/listing-photos/` with the numbered name shown (so the grid stays ordered).

## Two honest caveats
- Gemini may **redraw the map slightly** (soft street text, fuzzy coordinates) when it's small in the
  frame. That's fine for lifestyle shots seen at a distance. If you want the map perfectly crisp *and*
  framed, use `01-framed-whimsy.png` (already sharp) as your hero instead and use these for the ritual/room feel.
- Keep faces out or turned away — you can safely use your own family (hands, backs, side profiles).

## Reusable STYLE line (already baked into each prompt below, edit once if you want a different look)
> Warm natural morning daylight, soft shadows, cozy modern Scandinavian family home, airy and bright;
> muted palette of cream, sage green, soft coral and light oak; candid and authentic (not staged
> stock); shallow depth of field; photorealistic. Keep the uploaded poster's artwork, colours and
> title exactly as-is — do not restyle the map.

# Gemini Video Prompts — Motion Clips (Veo)

Short motion clips for **Pinterest video pins, Reels/TikTok, and the Etsy listing video** — the moving
version of the shots above. Generated on the **Gemini website → video (Veo)**.

## How to use
1. Open Gemini → **video / Veo**. If image-to-video is available, **upload the same framed/hero image**
   (e.g. `01-framed-whimsy.png`); otherwise describe the framed poster in the prompt.
2. Render **9:16 vertical** for Pinterest/Reels/TikTok; **1:1 or 4:5** for the Etsy listing video.
3. Clips run ~**8s**. Keep motion subtle. Add **text overlays + music later** in Canva/CapCut — and
   front-load pin keywords in the first line of the description (see `studio/brand/pinterest-guide.md`).
4. **Etsy listing video:** 5–15s, plays silently in-grid, no text needed — pick the calmest clip (V1/V4).

## Video caveats (read once)
- AI video can **warp the map** and **morph hands/faces**. Keep the map small or the motion minimal; for a
  crisp map or a genuine "watch me draw" clip, **film it** — a phone on a tripod is enough, and authentic
  raw process footage performs best on TikTok/Reels **and** reinforces Etsy's original-work standards.
- **No on-screen text or logos** from the model — add those yourself. Keep **faces out or turned away**.

*(Style baked into each prompt: warm morning daylight, cozy Scandinavian home, muted cream/sage/coral/oak,
slow premium camera motion, shallow focus, photorealistic, no text/logos/morphing.)*
