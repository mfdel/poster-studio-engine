# FAM-001 Playground Map — Phase 3: Market, Competition & Execution

> **Research base:** [research/research_playground_map_market_20260728.md](../../../studio/research/research_playground_map_market_20260728.md) (40 searches, 35 sources). Numbers below are cited or explicitly flagged as estimates.

> **One-line Phase 3 verdict:** **Genuine product-level white space on top of proven adjacent gift demand and workable margins — but a near-zero moat and *unproven playground-specific demand*. Score holds 18/25 🟡; 🟢 only as a lean, digital-first side hustle. The winning move is to sell it as a proven "personalized new-baby / nursery keepsake" and use "playground" as the differentiator — not to bet on "playground map" search demand.**

**Read:** this is a side-hustle-sized opportunity by construction — the founder already framed it that way. The market is big enough to matter, too commoditized and thin-moated to scale into a real company. Size the *joy + €1–3k/mo + low risk*, not a venture.

### A2. Demand signals — strong for the *gift*, unproven for the *playground*

- ✅ **New-parent / nursery gift demand is proven and large.** Individual personalized-nursery listings carry **58,900 / 35,400 / 24,700 / 6,200 reviews**; a safari nursery set did **8,158 sales / $758,707** ([Alura](https://www.alura.io/top-selling-etsy-items/prints)); **68% of expecting parents** want personalized name art first ([prettypicked](https://prettypicked.com/blog/etsy-nursery-decor)); adding a child's name **nearly doubles conversion** ([Insight Agent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/best-selling-printable-wall-art-etsy)).
- ✅ **Personalized map wall-art is a proven format** — multiple 7-year-old companies (see B) survive on it, one Etsy map-nursery listing did **5,059 sales / $55,644**.
- 🚩 **"Playground map" demand is *not* proven.** 40 searches found **no dedicated product and no keyword-volume evidence** for playground maps specifically. The demand we can bank on is *personalized-baby-gift* + *personalized-map* demand; "playground" is the **differentiator**, not the traffic source.
- ⚠️ **Etsy could not be verified directly** (all Etsy pages returned 403 / DataDome CAPTCHA). Demand figures above are from third-party Etsy analytics tools, not Etsy first-party — treat as directional.

**Strategic implication:** position and title the product against **proven** search demand ("personalized new baby gift," "nursery map print," "family keepsake map," "first birthday gift") and let the playground/annotation ritual be the *hook that differentiates* — do **not** rely on people searching "playground map."

### B2. General-LLM & AI-giant threat — LOW (but the *human* threat is the real one)

- **LLM threat: low (score 4).** The product is a physical/emotional keepsake + a family ritual; ChatGPT/Gemini can *list* playgrounds but cannot ship the artifact or the co-creation experience. This is *not* an intelligence-bottlenecked product, so foundation-model progress doesn't erode it.
- **The actual durability risk is human copycats — and it's real.** Data is free OSM, finder apps already expose 700k playgrounds, and **Grafomap is already an OSM-based map-poster company** that could add a playground layer in a day. There is **no data flywheel, no workflow lock-in, no regulatory barrier** — none of the 6 durable moats apply.
- **Only defensible assets:** (1) a **distinctive, ownable, kid-friendly illustrated art style** (incumbents are all minimalist/adult), (2) the **playground + family-ritual niche** nobody occupies today, (3) **gift positioning + brand voice**, (4) first-mover + authentic founder story. These protect a side hustle; they do not protect a scale business.

### B3. Gaps & wedge

Incumbents sell *generic adult street-map nostalgia* (where we met / travelled / got married). **None** do: playgrounds, a **child-facing** aesthetic, an **annotation/keepsake ritual**, or the **new-parent-gift** framing on a map. Positive Prints proves the digital tier works; nobody pairs digital + playground + gift.

**The wedge:** *the personalized-baby-gift keepsake, delivered as a playground map the child co-creates* — proven demand (nursery gifts) × unoccupied niche (playgrounds) × a differentiated art style incumbents won't copy without diluting their brand.

## C. Execution & validation

### C1. Unit economics (COGS = estimates; verify real Gelato per-size cost in the dashboard)

| SKU | Price | Est. COGS | Est. fees | **Contribution** | Margin | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Digital download** | €19 | ~€0 | ~€2.5 (Etsy) + VAT collected by platform | **~€13–15** | **~70–80%** | Scale/volume; near-zero ops |
| **POD poster 50×70** (Gelato) | €39 | ~€9–14 landed (local EU ship) | ~€4.5 | **~€14** | **~36%** | Mid-tier; zero inventory |
| **Framed poster** (Gelato) | €79 | ~€30–40 | ~€8 | **~€30–35** | ~40% | Premium |
| **Physical gift kit** | €69–89 | ~€25–40 (map + stickers + markers + booklet + box + ship) **+ your time** | ~€6 | ~€25–45 *(before time)* | variable | Gift tier — **fulfillment-heavy; add last** |

- **Gelato fit confirmed:** Sweden is a **local-production country** (32 countries, 85%+ printed in-country, 90% delivered ≤5 days), **GDPR-compliant, ISO 27001, EU data residency** ([local production](https://support.gelato.com/en/articles/8996076-where-do-you-print-locally) · [trust](https://www.gelato.com/trust)). Local EU shipping $3–5 vs $12–18 international — this is the lever that makes a Swedish seller viable globally.
- **The economics verdict:** **digital is where the money is clean; CAC is the swing factor.** A €14 poster contribution vanishes under paid ads → **no paid ads**; grow via organic Etsy SEO + Pinterest. Digital's 77% margin survives even modest promotion.
- ⚠️ **Exact Gelato per-size cost is behind the configurator** — the single number to verify before committing to poster pricing.

### C2. Go-to-market (first 100 sales)

1. **Etsy first** — borrow built-in gift-buyer traffic; no store to build, VAT partly handled by the platform. Title against **proven** keywords (personalized baby gift / nursery print / family keepsake), not "playground map."
2. **Pinterest** — the dominant channel for nursery decor + printables; pin the map mockups and the "choose a park together" ritual. Free, evergreen, printable-friendly.
3. **Founder story + parenting communities** — your authentic "I made this for my daughter" narrative in a few parenting FB groups / Instagram; this is your unfair advantage and it's free.
4. **Own Shopify later**, only once a repeatable seller exists (to escape Etsy fees + own the customer + upsell the kit).

### C3. Sweden legal / ops (lean start)

- **Company:** start as **enskild firma** with **F-skatt**; move to **aktiebolag** only if it scales. Low setup, low overhead.
- **VAT (moms):** 25% on both digital and goods; register for **EU VAT OSS** once cross-border B2C EU sales grow (Etsy collects/remits VAT in many jurisdictions — confirm per market).
- **Fulfillment:** **Gelato dropship** = you hold no stock, print+ship is automated and in-country → near-zero physical ops for the digital/poster tiers.
- **GDPR:** the only personal data is the address on the map (which *is* the product) + order/email. Keep it minimal, process via EU-based Gelato, don't retain beyond fulfillment. Low risk.
- **Licensing:** **OSM is ODbL** — attribution + share-alike. Workable (Mapiful/Grafomap both use OSM) but **add proper attribution** on prints and keep the styling as your own layer.
- **Payments:** Etsy Payments to start; Stripe/Klarna on Shopify later.

### C4. Riskiest-assumption test (do this *before* any art/dev investment)

**The riskiest assumption is demand for *this specific product*, not buildability.** Cheapest test:

1. List **one €15 digital "playground adventure map"** + **one €39 POD poster**, personalized to an address submitted in the order note, fulfilled **semi-manually** (you run the generator by hand). No web app, no kit, no ads.
2. Put up a **one-page landing + 5–10 Pinterest pins** driving to it.
3. Run **3 title/angle variants** to learn which pulls: *(a) personalized baby-gift, (b) family-adventure keepsake, (c) playground map.*
4. **Threshold to proceed:** e.g. **≥10–20 organic sales to strangers in 60–90 days**, with the *gift* angle converting. If yes → invest in art direction + a self-serve generator + the kit. If only downloads sell → stay digital-only and lean. If nothing sells → you still have the map you made for your daughter, at ~€0 sunk cost.

### C5. Now / Next / Later

- **Now (0–4 wks):** Build the one-city generator for your own daughter's map (you're doing this anyway) → it *is* the MVP + product photography. Nail a **distinctive art style** (the real differentiator). Verify **real Gelato COGS** for 50×70. Open enskild firma when first sale is near.
- **Next (1–3 mo):** Run the C4 smoke test on Etsy + Pinterest across the 3 angles. Measure which keyword/angle converts. Add OSM attribution.
- **Later (only if validated):** Self-serve generator front-end, own Shopify, the physical annotation **gift kit**, expand to strong-coverage geographies (DE 135k · UK 47k · FR 45k · NL 33k · SE 20k playgrounds — [taginfo](https://taginfo.openstreetmap.org/tags/leisure=playground)).

### Two decisions to carry forward
1. **Lead with the *gift*, not the playground.** Ride proven personalized-baby-gift demand; make "playground + child-co-created ritual" the differentiator. Betting on "playground map" search traffic is the way to sell zero.
2. **Digital-first, no paid ads, defer the kit.** Digital's ~77% margin is the only tier that survives promotion; poster margin dies under CAC; the physical kit is a fulfillment trap until demand is proven.
