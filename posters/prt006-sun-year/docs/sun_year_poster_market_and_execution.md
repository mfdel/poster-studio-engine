# PRT-006 Sun-Year Poster — Phase 3: Market, Competition & Execution

> **Research base:** ~30 web searches / fetches, 2026-08-03. Etsy first-party listing pages are bot-blocked (403/DataDome, confirmed pattern from FAM-001/FAM-002 Phase 3) — Etsy facts below come from search-snippet titles/descriptions only, labelled accordingly. > **One-line Phase 3 verdict:** **No one sells this exact poster — the ring, the twilight gradient, and the printed real sunrise time on a marked date are still unclaimed. But the category around it is not white space: a live programmatic-SEO company already sells "one exact place + one exact date" astro-adjacent prints at scale (Sideraform/SunriseWhen, €39), a UK shop already sells a personalized daylight-hours-over-the-year chart, and an Etsy seller already sells a sunrise/sunset print marked to a special date and titled for the exact same occasions PRT-006 wants to win. The star-map comparable holds up and is bigger than Phase 2 assumed (300,000+ units, a 20-year incumbent). Score holds at 17/25 🟡 — unchanged in number, materially sobered in substance: this is a real but thin style-level gap inside a crowded, well-precedented job, not an empty category.**

**Direct answer: no, the exact PRT-006 format — a filled ring on dark ground, twilight-graded bands, one marked day with its real printed sunrise time — is not sold anywhere found in this research.** That specific composition remains unclaimed. **But the job it does is not unclaimed:** the mechanism ("your exact coordinates + one meaningful date, rendered as automated data-art, sold as a physical gift print, €18–39") is already running as a live business (Sideraform), the *specific daylight-over-the-year data* is already a commercial product (Purest Green Prints), and the *specific emotional pitch* ("the sun that day, marked for you, for a birth/anniversary/wedding") is already a live Etsy listing. **PRT-006's genuine claim to novelty is the visual form (the ring, the twilight gradient) — a styling choice, not a category creation.** That is a real but narrow moat, and it is precisely the kind of gap a competent designer — human or AI-assisted — closes fastest, because the hard part (the astronomical data, the date-marking mechanic, the gift positioning) is already proven and copyable; only the rendering is left to imitate.

### B2. General-LLM & AI-giant threat — confirmed current, not hypothetical

- **Direct-LLM threat: still low-to-moderate (unchanged read).** A framed physical print delivered to a wall is not something ChatGPT ships; the emotional/gift context is not intelligence-bottlenecked.
- 🔴 **But the AI-assisted human-copycat threat Phase 2 flagged is now empirically documented as the current state of Etsy wall art in 2026, not a future risk.** Multiple 2026 sources confirm: "a flood of new sellers has uploaded generic designs into already-saturated categories, particularly wall art" ([Marmalead, 2026](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-print-on-demand/)); AI generators (Flux 2, Midjourney V7) now produce output "genuinely competitive with work from human digital artists" ([search synthesis, 2026 sources]); Etsy has responded with **stricter "creativity standards" enforcement specifically targeting AI-generated/dropshipped designs** ([iscompliant.app](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-creativity-standards-pod-sellers-guide)). This confirms — with 2026 evidence, not 2025 speculation — Phase 2's harshest self-criticism: **"cheapest build" and "lowest barrier to entry" are the same fact.** A competing seller with an AI image generator and an afternoon can produce a plausible imitation of the ring once it has any visibility.
- **What still protects PRT-006, narrowly:** it is not "AI art" in the sense Etsy is now policing (generic prompted images) — it is **generated from real computed astronomical data** (sunrise/sunset times via `astral`), which a copycat would need to *also* build, not just prompt for. That is a thin but real technical floor above pure image generation, though — as Phase 2 already admitted — it is ~30 lines of code, not a moat that survives a motivated cloner for more than an evening.
- **None of the 6 durable moats apply, confirmed.** The three defensible assets remain exactly what Phase 2 named: art direction/palette, Nordic-identity positioning and copy voice, and occasion/gift framing. All three are brand assets, not structural ones.

### B3. Gaps & the wedge

The wedge survives, but it is narrower than "nobody does this":

> **The specific combination — a ring/emblem shape (not a chart, not a coordinate grid), twilight-graded bands (not a flat curve), one marked day with its *actual printed sunrise time* (not just a date caption), sold with an explicit Nordic-identity/latitude-refusal brand voice — is real and currently unclaimed.** Every adjacent competitor found does *one* piece of this (the data, the date-marking, the occasion framing, the astro-gift positioning) but none combines all four into this shape. That is a genuine, if narrow, styling and positioning wedge — not a "no one has thought of this" wedge.

### C2. Go-to-market — first 100 customers, ranked for a time-poor solo founder

### C5. Riskiest-assumption test

Phase 2 named it precisely and this research does not change the diagnosis, only sharpens the test: **not buildability (confirmed cheap again), not wall-worthiness (already cleared) — whether the marked date is enough to make a beautiful shape feel personal to a stranger**, with the threshold **the dated listing must outsell the decorative one.**

Cheapest sequence, ordered by cost:
1. **€0, this weekend:** build the Malmö ring for the founder's own wall — it is the hero image, the product photo, and the founder story in one.
2. **€0, one evening:** post the hero image to Pinterest/Instagram with the founder's own story ("the sun rose at X the morning my daughter was born, here"). Measure saves/DMs — the cheapest available proxy for "I would hang that," and the same proxy FAM-002's Phase 3 used.
3. **List two Etsy digital SKUs (€19), one dated/occasion-framed, one undated/identity-framed**, titled to the proven occasion keywords from §C2, not to "sun ring" or "daylight poster." **Let the split decide the open question (Phase 2 §8 Q1): if the dated listing outsells the decorative one, the marked date is the product, as Phase 2 hypothesized; if not, the identity/decorative framing is carrying it and the "annotation IS the product" thesis needs revisiting.**
4. **Threshold to invest further:** the dated listing must outsell the decorative one, **and** combined organic sales must clear single digits per month within 60–90 days — otherwise this is a personal artefact, not a business, exactly as concluded for FAM-002's memorial map.
